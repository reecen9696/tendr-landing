## 5. Process

**Section heading:**
How it works

1. **Send the drawings.** Email us the tender package or share the EstimateOne link.
2. **Get a fixed fee.** We quote a price and turnaround date before you commit.
3. **We measure and price.** Full takeoff from the drawings, priced to your rates.
4. **You review.** Check the numbers, adjust your margin, tell us what to change.
5. **Submit.** Final tender formatted and ready to lodge.

## 8. FAQ

**How much does it cost?**
Fixed fee per tender, quoted up front once we've seen the package. Price depends on the size of the drawing set and the number of trades in scope. You'll know the cost before we start, and there's nothing to pay until you approve it.

**How fast can you turn a tender around?**
Most landscaping packages are back within [X] business days. Tight closing date? Tell us on the call and we'll confirm whether we can hit it before you commit.

**Do you use my rates or yours?**
Yours. We measure the quantities and structure the BOQ, then price it against your supplier costs and labour rates. The margin call is always yours to make.

**What do I actually receive?**
An itemised quantity takeoff, a priced bill of quantities in Excel, a scope of works with inclusions and exclusions, and a clarifications list for the builder. If the builder issued a pricing schedule, we complete it in their format.

**What if I only need the takeoff, not the pricing?**
Fine by us. Plenty of clients want measured quantities only and price it themselves. The fee drops accordingly.

**I'm not in landscaping. Can you still help?**
Landscaping is our core trade, and we also price civil, concreting and demolition packages. If your trade isn't listed, ask. If we can't measure it properly, we'll say so rather than guess.

**Who sees my pricing?**
Nobody. Your rates, suppliers and margins are covered by a confidentiality agreement and never shared between clients, including clients bidding the same job. If two clients ask us to price the same tender, we tell both and let you decide.

**Do I have to sign up to anything ongoing?**
No. Pay per tender. If you're bidding regularly and want us on call, we can set up an ongoing arrangement, but there's no lock-in either way.
