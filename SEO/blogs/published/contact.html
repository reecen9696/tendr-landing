Frequently asked questions

What should I bring to the call?

A live tender if you have one: the drawings, the scope and any pricing schedule the builder issued. We will tell you what it would cost to price, how long it would take, and whether it is worth bidding at all.

What does it cost to get a tender priced?

A fixed fee per tender, quoted up front once we have seen the package. The price depends on the size of the drawing set and the number of trades in scope. There is nothing to pay until you approve the quote.

Do I have to commit to anything ongoing?

No. You pay per tender, for the tenders you choose to bid. If you are bidding regularly and want us on call we can set that up, but there is no lock-in either way.
