What is different about pricing a Melbourne package

Melbourne is our home city, so the builders, the platforms and the tender formats are familiar ground. State work comes through Buying for Victoria, and most private commercial work arrives through EstimateOne or straight from the builder.

Much of Melbourne's growth corridor sits on the basalt plains, and reactive clay changes what an earthworks and subgrade line is worth. Where the geotechnical report is missing or the drawings do not say what is under the topsoil, that goes on the clarifications list rather than into a rate. Rock and unexpected ground conditions are the single most common source of a variation argument on a landscape package, and the cheapest place to deal with them is the tender.

The planting windows are the other thing that moves a price. Autumn and spring establishment is straightforward; a summer handover date means an irrigation and establishment allowance that a spring one does not, and a program that ignores that is a program that hands you a maintenance problem you priced nothing for.

Frequently asked questions

Do you work with Melbourne builders directly?

We work for the landscape subcontractor, not the builder. You stay the tenderer and you lodge in your own name. We do not build, and we never bid work in our own name.

Can you price work outside Melbourne?

Yes. We are based in Melbourne and work Australia-wide, because the work is done from the drawings rather than from the site.

Which Melbourne platforms do you work across?

Most private commercial work reaches us through EstimateOne or straight from the builder, and state work through the Victorian portal. The platform does not change the takeoff; it changes the format the submission has to come back in, and we match whatever was issued.
