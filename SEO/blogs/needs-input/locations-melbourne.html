What is included in a Melbourne tender price?

How does it work?

The process is built to fit around a tender deadline, not add to it. You send the documents, we price, you review and submit under your own name.

  1. Send us the drawings, scope and any pricing schedule (including tenders listed on EstimateOne).
  2. We confirm the fee and turnaround before starting, so there are no surprises.
  3. We complete the takeoff and priced BOQ, flagging any scope items that need a decision.
  4. You receive a submittable priced tender that maps to the head contractor's schedule.
