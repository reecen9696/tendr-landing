Services

What we price

Five services, all priced per tender, all against your own rates. Take one or take the lot.

  • Quantity takeoff

    A quantity takeoff measures every item in a tender scope off the drawings and itemises it line by line: areas, lengths, volumes and counts.

  • Priced bill of quantities

    A priced bill of quantities is the itemised schedule of a tender scope with a measured quantity, a unit and a rate on every line, totalling to the tender price.

  • Tender submission

    A tender submission is the complete package a builder asks for: the priced schedule, the scope of works, the inclusions and exclusions, and the clarifications list.

  • Scope and contract review

    A scope and contract review checks what you are actually agreeing to before you sign: the gap between what the drawings show and what the scope makes you responsible for, and the...

  • Variation pricing

    A variation is a change to the contracted scope, and it gets paid on the strength of its paperwork rather than the fairness of the claim.
