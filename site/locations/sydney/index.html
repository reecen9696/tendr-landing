Tender estimating in Sydney

Tendr prices commercial landscape tenders for Sydney subcontractors: the quantity takeoff off the drawings, the bill of quantities priced to your own rates, and the submission package the builder asked for. We are based in Melbourne and work Australia-wide, because the measuring is done from the drawings. Fixed fee per tender, quoted up front.

What we price

Five services, all priced per tender and all against your own rates: quantity takeoff, priced bill of quantities, tender submission, scope and contract review and variation pricing. Take one or take the lot. If you only want the measured quantities and intend to price them yourself, that is a common request and the fee drops accordingly. The services pages set out what each one covers and how the fixed fee works.

What is different about pricing a Sydney package

State work comes through NSW eTendering, council work through each council's own process, and most private commercial work through EstimateOne or the builder directly. The formats differ from Victoria's, and where a builder issues their own pricing schedule we complete it in their format rather than handing you something they have to re-key.

Sandstone is the Sydney-specific risk. A shelf that sits just under the topsoil turns a routine excavation rate into the largest variation on the job, and the drawings frequently do not show where it starts. Rock excavation gets qualified explicitly, with the assumption written down, because the difference between a priced allowance and an excluded item is the difference between a variation you get paid for and an argument you lose.

Access and site constraint are the other Sydney premium. Inner-suburban and CBD-adjacent work carries handling, staging and out-of-hours conditions that a greenfield rate does not cover. Those conditions are usually buried in the preliminaries and the conditions of contract rather than the landscape specification, which is why they get read before the package is priced.

Frequently asked questions

Are you based in Sydney?

No. We are based in Melbourne and work Australia-wide. The takeoff is measured from the drawings, so the location of the project does not change how the work is done or what it costs.

Do you handle rock excavation allowances?

We measure and price what the drawings and the geotechnical information support, and qualify the rest. Where rock is likely but undefined, it is flagged as an assumption or an exclusion rather than absorbed into a rate.

Do you price Sydney council work as well as state work?

Yes. Council packages come through each council's own process rather than one portal, and they vary more in format than state work does. We complete whatever schedule was issued.
